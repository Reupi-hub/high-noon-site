Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

High Noon (“the game”) is a 1v1 western duel game for iOS and Android, made by High Noon Games (“we”, “us”). This policy describes what data the game and this website collect, why, and what control you have over it. High Noon Games is responsible for this data. The browser version of the game is no longer available; this policy covers the mobile apps and high-noon.co.

Data we collect in the game

  • Account identity. You sign in with Google, with Apple (on iOS), or with an email address and a password. The name and email address your identity provider shares, or the address you type, are kept with your sign-in record in Google Firebase Authentication: they are not part of your game profile and other players never see them. With Apple’s “Hide My Email” you may share a private relay address instead of your real one — that works fine. With an email address, we send it a confirmation link; your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it. If you turn on two-step verification, the secret key of your authenticator app is kept by the same service.
  • Game profile and gameplay data. Your chosen username, battle tag, level, experience, in-game currencies, inventory, match history, rank and season statistics, achievements, bounty progress, Frontier Pass progress, and friend list.
  • Account history. Every change to your game profile and Frontier Pass is recorded in a history attached to your account: what changed (for example gold, wampum, experience, level, rank or items), the value before and after, and the action that caused it (a duel result, a reward, a purchase). We use it to answer support requests, give back anything lost to a bug, and detect cheating and fraud. It is deleted with your account.
  • Season records. When a season ends, and when the beta ended, we keep a copy of your game profile at that moment, so that rewards and items from past seasons can be returned to you. They are deleted with your account.
  • Online presence. Whether you are currently online, in the lobby, or in a duel — shown to other players (e.g. in the lobby carousel and friend list).
  • Device motion (gyroscope). The app uses the gyroscope to aim during duels. Motion data is processed on your device only and is never transmitted or stored.
  • Player reports and blocks. If you report or block another player, we store the report (who reported whom, the reason, and an optional note) or your block list, to moderate the game and keep abusive players away from you.
  • Bug reports. If you send a bug report, we store what you write, the category, your player ID, username and battle tag, the app version, device model, OS version and language, the screen or duel concerned, and the app session ID (to match the report with the analytics events below). We use it only to fix the game and, during the beta, to award bug-bounty rewards. A short notice without your name or your text (report number, category, app version, device model) is posted in our team’s private Discord channel. Bug reports are deleted with your account, and at the latest 12 months after the game’s official launch.

Notifications

Game reminders (a new daily bounty, a bounty about to end, Sundown Showdown, energy back to full, Frontier Pass reminders) are local notifications: the app schedules them on your phone. We do not collect a push token, and no server sends them. You can turn each one off in the app (Menu → Settings → Notifs), or all of them in your phone’s settings.

Diagnostics & analytics

  • Crash and error reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report (error message, stack trace, the screen you were on, device model, OS version, app version, and your player ID so we can help with support requests) is stored in our own database, with the analytics below. It is not sent to any third party. No advertising identifiers are collected.
  • Gameplay analytics. We log product events first-party into our own database (screens visited, buttons tapped, sign-in method, match funnel and duel statistics such as draw time, accuracy and smoothness, device model and OS version, the region and time zone set on your phone, and the response time of our servers) to improve the game and tune fair-play protections. This data is never shared with third parties.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising inside the game, and no advertising or tracking SDK in the app.
  • No sale of your personal data.

Where your data lives

Game data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database) on servers in the European Union (Belgium and France). Sign-in records are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, a Google service that may process them outside the European Union. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Purchases

In the app, the Frontier Pass and wampum packs are sold through the App Store or Google Play, which process the payment. Our purchase-validation provider RevenueCat receives the purchase record (product, transaction ID, date, store) linked to your player ID, so the purchase can be credited to your account and the pass restored on a new device. We never receive your payment details.

The former browser version sold optional items through Stripe, and the “Support the devs” link on our beta page opens a Stripe payment page. Stripe processes those payments; we keep a record of the transaction (not card details) for legal and accounting purposes.

Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase — game servers, database and sign-in.
  • Google and Apple sign-in — to identify your account.
  • Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat — purchases (see above).
  • Discord — our community server is a third-party service with its own privacy policy. We only see what you choose to post there.
  • Mailchimp, Meta (the Meta Pixel) and GitHub Pages — this website (see below).

This website

  • “Notify me” form. If you leave your email address, it is stored by Mailchimp (a US service, with its own privacy policy). We use it only to tell you when the game is out — the launch telegram. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove your address.
  • Quick-draw game. Your best times are kept in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to us.
  • Meta Pixel. The site uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads and to show High Noon ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who visited it. You can turn it off at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the home page.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Menu → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, match history, account history, season records, inventory, friends, friend requests and challenges, bug reports, analytics and crash reports, presence data, and sign-in record. Some non-personal records are retained where required (payment records for legal/accounting purposes, and anonymized anti-cheat audit entries).

No longer have the app? See how to request deletion without the app.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop a use of it you disagree with. Write to contact@high-noon.co from the address linked to your sign-in. If you are in the European Union, you can also complain to your data-protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also announced in the game or on Discord.

Contact

Questions or data requests: join our Discord or email contact@high-noon.co.
