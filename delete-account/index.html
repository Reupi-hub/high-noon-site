Delete your account

High Noon, for iOS and Android, by LEMON TREE.

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open High Noon and sign in.
  2. Tap the round menu button in the top-right corner of the town square, then Settings.
  3. Open the Account tab and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app

Email contact@high-noon.co with the subject “Account deletion request”, from the email address linked to your Google or Apple sign-in. Include your in-game username or battle tag if you know it. We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, username and battle tag, match history, account history, season records, inventory and in-game currencies, Frontier Pass progress, friends and pending friend requests or challenges, bug reports, online presence data, and your sign-in record.

What is kept

Only records we must keep: payment records for legal and accounting purposes, and anonymized anti-cheat audit entries that no longer identify you. Analytics events keep only the random player ID, which no longer points to anyone once the account is gone.

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases made through the App Store or Google Play — those are handled by the store.

More details in our privacy policy.
