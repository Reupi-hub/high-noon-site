What you'll test

The whole game.

Not a slice of it. Everything that's live in the app, with new builds landing often.

  1. I

    Real-time 1v1 duels

    Draw, aim with your phone, shoot, reload. Against players from everywhere — or a bot when the street is empty.

  2. II

    Three guns, three finishes

    Schofield, Navy, Shotgun — in Steel, Silver and Gold.

  3. III

    Ranked seasons

    Every duel moves your Reward. Five divisions, Rookie to Legend. The top guns of each season win exclusive skins.

  4. IV

    Daily bounties

    A new challenge every day. Keep the streak alive for bigger rewards.

  5. V

    Skins, items, Battle Pass

    Outlaw skins, potions, dynamite, elixirs. Skins from the browser alpha are already in your locker.

  6. VI

    Friends & Call Outs

    Add friends, see who's in town, and call anyone out to a friendly duel.

  7. VII

    Bosses, achievements, Mystery Bags

    Boss duels, achievements to hunt, and the odd Mystery Bag after a win.

  8. VIII

    Leaderboard & ledger

    See where you stand worldwide. Every duel is in your ledger: gold, XP, Reward swing.

How it works

Beta, not a demo.

  • 01

    iPhone, via TestFlight

    Testers get an invitation by email, install the app with TestFlight and play the real game, with real players.

  • 02

    New builds, often

    Fixes and features ship as they're ready. Testers report in #beta-feedbacks on Discord — every report counts.

  • 03

    Android is next

    The Android beta is coming. The same waitlist covers both platforms.
