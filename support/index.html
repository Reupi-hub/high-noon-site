Getting started

How do I sign in?

With your Google account, with Apple on iPhone, or with an email address and a password. Your progress is saved on our servers and tied to that sign-in.

I have a new phone. How do I get my progress back?

Install High Noon and sign in the same way you did before (the same Google or Apple account, or the same email address). Everything comes back: level, rank, gold, wampum and items.

Which phones can play?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, and Android 7.0 or later. The phone needs a gyroscope, since you aim by moving it — almost every phone has one. High Noon is an online game, so you also need an internet connection.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your phone’s language when it’s English, French or Arabic, and uses English otherwise. To change it, open the menu (round button, top right of the town square), then Settings, and tap the flag button at the bottom.

Duels

How do I aim and shoot?

Move your phone to aim: the crosshair follows it. Tap anywhere on the screen to shoot. To reload, tilt your phone down, then tap the cylinder. Aim for the head!

What is the “Lower your gun” gauge before the bell?

That’s the holster check. Point your phone at the ground, like a revolver at your hip, and hold it in the green until the bell rings. If your phone is out of the green when the bell rings, you lose 25% of your health.

The crosshair doesn’t move, or moves too much.

Close the app completely and open it again. You can change the aiming sensitivity, or invert the axes, in Menu → Settings → Controls. If it still doesn’t work, report a bug from the app with your phone model.

Nobody to duel?

When you tap FIND DUEL, the game looks for a gunslinger close to your level for up to 25 seconds. If nobody is free, you face a computer-controlled outlaw instead, so you never wait longer than that.

How does energy work?

Each duel costs 1 energy point, taken when the duel ends. You hold up to 8 points and get 1 back every 10 minutes — a full bar in 80 minutes. An Energy Potion refills the bar at once, and the game can send you a reminder when your energy is full again.

Frontier Pass & purchases

What is the Frontier Pass?

A season-long reward track: duels, quests and daily gifts move you up its tiers. The free track is open to everyone. Premium and Premium+ add exclusive skins and more rewards, and are bought through the App Store or Google Play.

I bought the pass but don’t have it.

Open the Frontier Pass, open the upgrade offer and tap Restore purchases under the buy button. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account and the same High Noon account you used to pay. Still missing? Email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds do too: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android from your order history in Google Play.

What are the odds in a saddlebag?

Saddlebags hold random rewards. Tap Odds & contents in the game to see the odds of each rarity and what a bag can contain before you open it.

Bugs & other players

How do I report a bug?

In the app: Menu → Settings → Support → Report a Bug. Pick the kind of bug, describe what happened and send. You get a report number: post your screenshots or videos with that number in #bug-reports on our Discord. You can also report a problem from a duel’s result screen, or right after a crash.

During the beta, confirmed bugs earn wampum, paid at launch — the bug-bounty rules are pinned on Discord. Found a cheat or an exploit? Pick “Cheat / exploit (private)” and keep it to yourself.

How do I report or block a player?

Open the player’s profile and tap Report or Block. You can see and unblock the players you blocked in Menu → Settings → Account → Blocked Players.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Menu → Settings → Account → Delete Account. No longer have the app? Request it by email.
